Professional-grade diagnostic instrumentation brought directly to your clinical office environment.

Spirometry

Pre- and post-bronchodilator testing to assess airflow obstruction, monitor chronic conditions like COPD and asthma, and evaluate dyspnea with high precision.

Lung Volumes

Precise measurement of functional residual capacity and total lung capacity to diagnose restrictive pulmonary disorders accurately on-site.

Diffusion Capacity (DLCO)

Evaluation of the alveolar-capillary membrane's efficiency in gas transfer to support complex differential diagnoses without patient travel.
