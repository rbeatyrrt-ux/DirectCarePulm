Distinguished Military & Healthcare Leadership

Robert Beaty is a U.S. Army veteran whose career spans over 20 years of high-level clinical practice, academic curriculum design, and healthcare administration. Having served as Chief of Respiratory Care Services at the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (West Palm Beach VAMC) and managed large multi-FTEE clinical departments, Robert brings institutional-grade operational rigor and compliance oversight to ambulatory diagnostics.

National Consulting & Academic Impact

As the Founder of AeroHealth Consultant Services, Robert has served as a national cardiopulmonary expert consulting for elite institutions including the Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, and the University of Miami School of Medicine, where he delivered resident education in advanced ventilator management. Furthermore, he shares his expertise nationally as an adjunct faculty member for the Bachelor of Science in Respiratory Therapy (BSRT) program at Nova Southeastern University.

Our Mission for Ohio Clinics

DirectCare Pulmonary Diagnostics was built to solve a glaring operational bottleneck in primary care: the barriers that prevent patients from receiving timely pulmonary function testing. By combining cutting-edge, cabinless MiniBox+ technology with expert RRT mobile execution, Robert and his team empower independent primary care providers to retain clinical control, improve patient outcomes, and capture ancillary revenue.

SDVOSB Commitment: Operating as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, DirectCare embodies the core military values of precision, uncompromising safety, and dedicated service to the West Central and Northwest Ohio healthcare community.
