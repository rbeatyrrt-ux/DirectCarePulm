Military Veteran & Healthcare Leadership

As a U.S. Army veteran and seasoned healthcare administrator holding a Doctorate and Master's degree in Healthcare Administration (DHA/MHA), Robert Beaty brings decades of rigorous clinical and operational expertise to ambulatory care. His background encompasses leadership as a Supervisory Respiratory Therapist, academic curriculum design in advanced respiratory care, and hands-on clinical practice.

Our Mission for Rural Ohio

DirectCare Pulmonary Diagnostics was founded on a simple premise: high-quality diagnostic testing should not depend on geography. By taking advantage of mobile commercial transport and advanced cabinless diagnostic technology, we bridge the gap between primary care physicians and specialized pulmonary evaluations.

SDVOSB Commitment: Operating as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, we embody the core values of integrity, dedication, and service to our local communities and partner clinics.
