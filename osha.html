Mandatory Standard

Meeting OSHA 1910.134 Mandates

Under the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) Respiratory Protection Standard (29 CFR 1910.134), employers must provide a medical evaluation to determine an employee’s ability to use a respirator before fit testing or field deployment.

DirectCare provides expert, RRT-supervised pulmonary assessments and spirometry evaluations designed to satisfy compliance mandates for industrial, manufacturing, and construction workforces across West Central Ohio.

Key OSHA Compliance Benefits:

  • ✓Compliant baseline spirometry testing (FVC, FEV1)
  • ✓Physician review and medical clearance documentation
  • ✓Minimized employee downtime with streamlined on-site scheduling
