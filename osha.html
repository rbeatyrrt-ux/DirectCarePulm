Meeting OSHA 1910.134 Mandates

Under 29 CFR 1910.134, employers must provide medical evaluations to determine an employee’s ability to use a respirator before fit testing. DirectCare delivers RRT-supervised baseline spirometry and clearance workflows directly to industrial facilities.
