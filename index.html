State-of-the-Art Equipment

The PulmOne MiniBox+ Advantage

Revolutionizing pulmonary function testing by removing cumbersome 600-pound hospital body boxes.

Gasless Lung Volumes in 60 Seconds

Traditional hospital labs rely on heavy nitrogen washout or helium dilution tanks. The MiniBox+ utilizes advanced aerodynamic plethysmography to capture complete Total Lung Capacity (TLC), Functional Residual Capacity (FRC), and Residual Volume (RV) through calm tidal breathing.

Compact enough for standard tabletop placement, our mobile setup requires zero permanent facility modification.
