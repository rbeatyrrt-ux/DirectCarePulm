Flexible, Frictionless Diagnostic Overreads

Generate supplementary income by providing asynchronous diagnostic interpretations for rural clinics, backed by our corporate malpractice rider and pristine ATS/ERS-compliant data.

Why DirectCare?

High-Quality Data. Zero Admin Headaches.

The biggest frustration for pulmonologists doing remote overreads is poor data quality from untrained medical assistants, and the hassle of navigating multiple clunky hospital EMRs.

DirectCare eliminates both. Every test is personally executed by a Board-Certified Respiratory Therapist (RRT) using the ndd EasyOne Pro LAB (ultrasonic TrueFlow). You review perfect, artifact-free flow-volume loops in a single, unified AWS portal.

Contractor Benefits

Competitive Per-Read Compensation

Earn $30 for full PFT suites (Spiro, DLCO, Volumes) and $15 for standalone spirometry. Automated month-end settlements—no invoicing required.

Malpractice Rider Included

You do not need to amend your primary malpractice policy. Our Corporate Professional Liability policy includes a secondary 1099 rider covering your remote interpretations.

Asynchronous Freedom

Log into the portal when you have downtime. You have a generous 24 to 48-hour window to complete your interpretations.
