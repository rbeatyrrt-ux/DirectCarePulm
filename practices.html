Turn Referrals Into Retained Revenue

Stop losing global diagnostic billing to massive hospital networks. DirectCare brings hospital-grade testing into your clinic with zero capital risk and zero maintenance overhead.

The ROI Equation

A Zero-Risk Ancillary Revenue Stream

In the traditional model, referring a patient out for a Pulmonary Function Test means the specialist's hospital network captures 100% of the diagnostic revenue.

With DirectCare's "PFT Clinic Day" model, the testing is performed inside your medical home. Your practice bills the global CPT codes directly to the payer. You simply pay DirectCare a flat, fee-for-service rate per test. The remaining margin stays in your practice.

Average Retained Net Margin: $85.00+ per patient tested.

1. Global Billing Clinic Bills Payer
2. Overhead Flat Fee to DirectCare
3. Net Profit Clinic Retains Margin

Why buying your own equipment is a financial liability.

Capital Expenditure (CapEx)

Purchasing In-House Equipment

  • ✕ $35,000+ Upfront: Massive initial capital required to purchase a clinical-grade plethysmography cabin.
  • ✕ $1,500+ Annual Maintenance: Expensive mandatory service contracts and software updates.
  • ✕ Staffing Overhead: Hiring a dedicated, credentialed Respiratory Therapist (RRT) or paying to train existing nursing staff.
  • ✕ Overread Fees: You still have to pay an outside pulmonologist to interpret the data.
The Smart Solution
Operational Expenditure (OpEx)

The DirectCare Partnership

  • ✓ $0 Upfront Cost: We bring the $35,000+ PulmOne MiniBox+ directly into your clinic.
  • ✓ $0 Maintenance: We handle all device calibration, software updates, and consumable supplies (filters, nose clips).
  • ✓ Expert Staffing Provided: Our Board-Certified RRT executes the testing. Your nurses stay focused on clinic flow.

Seamless Integration

How a "PFT Clinic Day" Works

1

Schedule

Your staff aggregates symptomatic patients and schedules them sequentially for a dedicated half-day or full-day block.

2

Arrival

Our RRT arrives with the MiniBox+, sets up in a standard exam room, and executes testing with zero disruption to your clinic flow.

3

Overread

Data is securely transmitted to our pulmonology network. A signed diagnostic report is routed back to your EMR within 48 hours.

4

Revenue

You bill the global CPT codes. We invoice you our flat rate at the end of the month. You retain the clinical control and the margin.
