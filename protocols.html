Standard Operating Procedures

Clinical Protocols & Quality Assurance

Rigorous operational standards governing testing methodology, infection control, and diagnostic compliance for our B2B clinical partners.

SOP-01

Pulmonary Function Testing

Establishes uniform instructions for patient preparation, contraindication screening (such as recent myocardial infarction, eye surgery, or aortic aneurysms), and procedure execution under RRT supervision. Ensures reproducible tidal breathing measurements and maximal forced exhalation maneuvers.

View Full SOP
SOP-02

Infection Control & Bioburden

Mandates the strict deployment of single-use, disposable 0.025-micron inline viral/bacterial filter mouthpieces achieving >99.999% filtration efficiency. Outlines clinical surface disinfection protocols between patient encounters to ensure full compliance with CDC ambulatory safety guidelines.

View Full SOP
SOP-03

ATS / ERS Calibration Compliance

Governs daily equipment calibration checks using certified 3-Liter calibration syringes, ambient temperature/barometric pressure logging, and strict adherence to American Thoracic Society (ATS) and European Respiratory Society (ERS) acceptability and repeatability criteria.

View Full SOP
SOP-04

Data Security & EMR Transmission

Outlines HIPAA-compliant handling of Protected Health Information (PHI), end-to-end encrypted file transfers via AWS S3 secure infrastructure, and structured 24–48 hour pulmonologist overread integration directly into partner clinic EMR systems.

View Full SOP

The DirectCare Clinical Guarantee

All standard operating procedures are strictly managed and enforced by a Board-Certified Registered Respiratory Therapist (RRT) holding advanced critical care (ACCS) and pulmonary function testing (CPFT/RPFT) national credentials. Every diagnostic data set submitted to our Pulmonology network is guaranteed to meet ATS/ERS acceptability criteria.
