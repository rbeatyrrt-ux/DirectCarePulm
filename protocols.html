Standard Operating Procedures

Clinical Protocols & Quality Assurance SOPs

Rigorous operational standards governing testing methodology, infection control, and calibration compliance.

SOP-01

Standard Operating Procedure: Pulmonary Function Testing

Establishes uniform instructions for patient preparation, contraindication screening (such as recent myocardial infarction, eye surgery, or aortic aneurysms), and procedure execution under RRT supervision. Ensures reproducible tidal breathing measurements and maximal forced exhalation maneuvers.

SOP-02

Standard Operating Procedure: Infection Control & Bioburden

Mandates the strict deployment of single-use, disposable 0.025-micron inline viral/bacterial filter mouthpieces achieving >99.999% filtration efficiency. Outlines clinical surface disinfection protocols between patient encounters to ensure full compliance with CDC ambulatory safety guidelines.

SOP-03

Standard Operating Procedure: ATS / ERS Compliance & Calibration

Governs daily equipment calibration checks, ambient temperature/barometric pressure logging, and adherence to American Thoracic Society (ATS) and European Respiratory Society (ERS) acceptability and repeatability criteria.

SOP-04

Standard Operating Procedure: Data Security & EMR Transmission

Outlines HIPAA-compliant handling of Protected Health Information (PHI), encrypted file transfers, and structured 24–48 hour pulmonologist overread integration directly into partner clinic EMR systems.
