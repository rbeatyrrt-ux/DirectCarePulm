Evolution of PFT

Why Cabinless Plethysmography?

For decades, measuring absolute lung volumes—specifically Total Lung Capacity (TLC) and Residual Volume (RV)—required enclosing patients inside a rigid, claustrophobic 600-pound glass body box. While clinically accurate, these legacy units are prohibitively expensive, immobile, and logistically impossible for independent primary care offices.

The PulmOne MiniBox+ replaces the physical cabin with advanced acoustic and aerodynamic algorithms, capturing true physiological lung volumes through effortless tidal breathing.

Uncompromising Accuracy

The Three Pillars of Testing

Fully compliant with American Thoracic Society (ATS) and European Respiratory Society (ERS) standardization.

1. Gasless Lung Volumes

TLC, FRC, and RV via Aerodynamics

Measures thoracic gas volume and absolute lung capacities in just 60 seconds of calm tidal breathing. Eliminates errors associated with incomplete gas dilution (helium washout) or claustrophobia-induced hyperventilation.

Clinical Impact: Essential for diagnosing restrictive lung disease and differentiating COPD phenotypes.
2. Full Spirometry

Pre/Post Bronchodilator (CPT 94060)

Precise measurement of FVC, FEV1, FEV1/FVC ratios, and forced expiratory flow parameters. Automated quality-control feedback ensures every maneuver meets rigorous ATS repeatability criteria before acceptance.

Clinical Impact: Gold standard for obstruction grading and reversibility testing.
3. DLCO Diffusion

Single-Breath Diffusing Capacity

Quantifies the lungs' ability to transfer gas from alveolar air into the pulmonary capillary blood supply. Crucial for early detection of interstitial lung disease, pulmonary vascular disorders, and emphysema.

Clinical Impact: Identifies alveolar-capillary membrane impairment long before resting hypoxia manifests.
